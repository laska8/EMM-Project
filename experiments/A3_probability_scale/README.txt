A3: coefficient versus probability scale

The reference AME was -4.44 percentage points. AMEs for the fixed top ten ranged from -9.01 to -5.59 percentage points. All ten subgroup-reference differences had the same direction on the coefficient and probability scales. However, the Spearman correlation between rankings by absolute coefficient difference and absolute AME difference was only 0.370. Directional conclusions were consistent, while the ranking of substantive effect magnitudes depended on the scale.
