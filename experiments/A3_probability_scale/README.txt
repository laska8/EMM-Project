A3: coefficient versus probability scale

The reference AME was -4.44 percentage points. AMEs for the fixed top ten ranged from -10.14 to -7.25 percentage points. 10 of the 10 subgroup-reference differences had the same direction on the coefficient and probability scales. The Spearman correlation between rankings by absolute coefficient difference and absolute AME difference was -0.697, which shows how far the ordering of effect magnitudes depends on the scale.
