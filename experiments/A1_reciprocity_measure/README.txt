A1: binary versus proportional reciprocity

Binary and proportional reciprocity retained nine of the ten highest-quality subgroup descriptions (Jaccard overlap 9/11 = 0.818). All nine shared top-ten subgroups had negative coefficients and more negative coefficients than their own measure-specific references. Across all 29 shared groups, coefficient signs agreed in 29/29 cases, while the direction of the subgroup-reference difference agreed in 21/29. Thus the main selected contexts and association signs were broadly stable, but ranking and some reference-relative conclusions depended on the definition of reciprocity. Weighted reciprocity is not applicable to the supplied adjacency data; see audits/weighted_feasibility.json.
