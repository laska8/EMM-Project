A2: ordinary versus user-clustered uncertainty

User clustering increased the reference standard error by 13.1% and top-ten subgroup standard errors by 2.1%-10.8% (median 5.9%). All ten subgroup confidence intervals remained below zero under both approaches. Coefficients and the quality ranking were unchanged. M already used user-clustered inference; A2 supplies its explicit comparison with ordinary model-based uncertainty.
